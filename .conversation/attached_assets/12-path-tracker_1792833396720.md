After the hackathon. Build ZeroCarbon Path.

For each seller project, a vertical tracker of the 15 steps: 1 Mark the land, 2 ZeroCarbon Scan, 3 Fill the gaps, 4 Choose a route, 5 Sign the agreement, 6 ICM account, 7 Project design document, 8 Validation, 9 Registration, 10 Plant and monitor, 11 Verification, 12 Issuance, 13 List on ZeroCarbon Market, 14 Sale, 15 Get paid.

- Each step has a status (not started, in progress, blocked, done), who acts next, and a note.
- Steps 6 to 12 are updated from /admin for now.
- Steps 10 to 15 repeat for every monitoring period: add a period selector.
- A "What now?" button calls AGENT_ID_PATH_COORDINATOR and shows next_action, who, and any deadline warning.

Add AGENT_ID_PATH_COORDINATOR to Secrets first.
