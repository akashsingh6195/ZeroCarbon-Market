Build the Market.

- Listing cards: project name; the labels as chips (market, action, status, vintage, method and registry); ZeroCarbon Score out of 100; price per tonne; quantity; delivery year for Future listings; the Demo data chip.
- Filters: market, action, status, vintage from, max price, region.
- Traders only ever see market = voluntary. Obligated buyers only see india_compliance. Hidden listings never show.
- Listing page: a map pin (Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles), the description, labels, score and a Buy button.
- Compare: tick up to 3 listings and show them side by side: price, score, vintage, status, delivery.
- One route for the models service, not for people: GET /api/internal/listings, read-only, returns all visible listings as JSON, and only answers when the header X-Internal-Key equals the INTERNAL_KEY secret.

Check before moving on: signed in as a trader, no Indian offset or compliance listing shows anywhere; /api/internal/listings refuses a call without the key.
