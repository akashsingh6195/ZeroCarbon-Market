Build a mobile-first web app called "ZeroCarbon Market" by ZeroCarbon: a marketplace for Indian carbon credits.

Stack: React + TypeScript + Tailwind on the front end, Node + Express on the back end, Replit PostgreSQL for data. Every call to an outside service goes through an Express route; the browser never sees an API key.

Design:
- Phone first: works at 375 px wide, no sideways scrolling, tap targets at least 44 px.
- Colours: Forest Green #1E5B3A (brand, main buttons), Leaf #6FAE4F (removal credits), Marigold #E9A23B (calls to action, "Future" tags), Registry Blue #2F4B7C (Indian government credits), Soil #7A5A3A (seller screens), Paper #F7F5EE (background), Ink #1D2421 (text).
- Fonts: Inter, with Noto Sans Devanagari and Noto Sans Kannada as fallbacks.
- One reusable "Demo data" chip, shown on every page that shows credits, prices or certificates.
- A bottom navigation bar: Market, Scan, Orders, Ask.
- Plain words everywhere. Never use the words invest, returns, yield, profit or guaranteed. "ZeroCarbon" is only our name: never tell a user they are zero carbon, carbon neutral or net zero.

Home page: the line "Know what you're buying. Know what your land can earn." and two buttons, "Buy credits" and "Check my land".

Check before moving on: the home page opens on a phone with no sideways scrolling, and the Demo data chip is one shared component.
