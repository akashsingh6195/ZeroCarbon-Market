---
name: Revenue principles
description: Business constraints for future ZeroCarbon pricing and monetization work.
---

ZeroCarbon's launch prices are proposed, not validated prices; review them after the first 20 to 30 paying customers. Free land eligibility checks must remain useful even when the answer is negative. Do not charge farmers upfront for uncertain credits or payouts.

The marketplace deal fee is seller-side and earned only after delivery, never added to the buyer's price. Official registry fees stay at actual cost, independent audits stay separate, and credit value traded is not ZeroCarbon revenue. Preserve compliance buying restrictions and voluntary-only trader access.

Plan and service buttons in this demo only record interest; they do not take or simulate payment. The user subsequently added one exception: professional land-review booking opens their external Google Form, where the QR payment and customer details are handled. The website collects nothing for this booking. Offer it on the landowner pricing card and only for likely-eligible or needs-checks Karnataka scans, never on a not-eligible scan.

**Why:** The user specified these protections and the proposed launch revenue model.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints when changing pricing, checkout, landowner offers, or revenue reporting. Read numeric prices and rates from the shared pricing configuration rather than copying them into UI text.
